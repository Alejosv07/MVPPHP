Cancellation & Quality Guarantee

Last updated: November 8, 2026

We keep our schedule tight so our teams arrive on time. These rules are fair to you and to the cleaners who reserve their day for your home.

1. Cancelling or rescheduling

When you tell usFee
48 hours or more before the appointmentFree
Less than 48 hours before50% of the booked price
Same day, or lock-out (see below)100% of the booked price

Notice time is counted from when we receive your message. The easiest ways to cancel or reschedule are the Cancel and Reschedule buttons in our emails, or replying to bookings@luxuriapure.com. You can also call or text +1 (443) 535-7785.

Requests that have not been confirmed yet (no quote accepted) can be cancelled at any time at no cost.

2. Lock-outs

A lock-out happens when our team arrives within the scheduled window and cannot start the service within 20 minutes because of no access (wrong code, no key, no building access), no utilities, or unsafe conditions. We will try to reach you by phone and text during that time. A lock-out is charged as a same-day cancellation.

3. If we need to cancel

If we must cancel because of severe weather, an emergency or staff illness, there is no fee and we will offer you the next available date. If we cancel less than 24 hours before your appointment, you get priority in rebooking.

4. Recurring services

You can skip, move or pause a recurring visit with at least 48 hours' notice at no cost. You can end a recurring plan at any time by email; visits already within the 48-hour window follow the fees above.

5. Quality guarantee

  • If any area included in your service is not up to standard, contact us within 24 hours of the visit at bookings@luxuriapure.com, ideally with photos.
  • We will return to re-clean those specific areas at no charge, usually within 3 business days.
  • Please give us the chance to re-clean before requesting a refund. If the re-clean still does not meet the standard, we will offer a partial or full refund for the affected part of the service.
  • The guarantee covers tasks included in your service. It does not cover tasks excluded in our Terms of Service, areas we could not access, or conditions we told you could not be fully resolved (for example, permanent stains).

6. Holds, charges and refunds

  • An authorization hold may be placed on your card the day before the service. It is released if you cancel with 48 hours' notice or more.
  • Refunds are issued to the original payment method. Banks usually take 5–10 business days to show them.
  • If you believe a charge is wrong, contact us first at bookings@luxuriapure.com; we will review it within 5 business days.

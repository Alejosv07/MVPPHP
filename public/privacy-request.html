Your Privacy Choices

Last updated: November 8, 2026

You control your personal information. This page explains how to make a privacy request to Luxuria Pure. It applies to everyone, and it meets the requirements of the Maryland Online Data Privacy Act (MODPA), the Virginia Consumer Data Protection Act (VCDPA) and the laws of the District of Columbia. For the full details of what we collect and why, read our Privacy Policy.

We do not sell or share your data for advertising

We do not sell personal information, we do not use it for targeted advertising, and we do not use it for profiling that produces legal or similarly significant effects. We also honor Global Privacy Control (GPC) signals sent by your browser as an opt-out request, even though there is nothing to opt out of today. If this ever changes, we will update this page and ask for your consent where the law requires it.

How to make a request

  1. Email privacy@luxuriapure.com (or call +1 (443) 535-7785) from the email address you used to book with us.
  2. Tell us the request type and, if it helps, your reservation number (for example #RES-0123). Please do not send ID documents or card numbers by email.
  3. We send a verification code to the email address in our records. Requests are completed only after verification, to protect your information from someone pretending to be you.

Requests are free of charge up to twice in any 12-month period. We may decline or charge a reasonable fee for requests that are manifestly unfounded, excessive or repetitive, and we will tell you why.

How long it takes

We respond within 45 days of receiving your request. If we reasonably need more time, we may extend this once by another 45 days and will tell you within the first 45 days.

Authorized agents

Someone else can make a request for you if they send us your signed written permission (or a valid power of attorney). We may still ask you to verify your identity directly with us. A parent or legal guardian may make requests on behalf of a minor.

If you disagree with our answer (appeals)

If we decline all or part of your request, you can appeal by replying to our answer, or by writing to privacy@luxuriapure.com with the subject "Privacy Appeal". Someone who was not involved in the original decision will review it. We answer appeals within 60 days and explain the reasons. If your appeal is denied, you can contact:

  • Maryland: Office of the Attorney General, Consumer Protection Division — marylandattorneygeneral.gov
  • Virginia: Office of the Attorney General, Consumer Protection Section — oag.state.va.us
  • District of Columbia: Office of the Attorney General, Office of Consumer Protection — oag.dc.gov

No discrimination for using your rights

We will never deny service, charge a different price or provide a different quality of service because you exercised a privacy right.

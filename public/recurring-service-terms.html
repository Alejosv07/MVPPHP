Recurring Service Terms

Last updated: November 8, 2026

These terms apply when you choose a weekly, bi-weekly or monthly cleaning plan. They are part of our Terms of Service. A recurring plan is a continuous service: it keeps going until you cancel it, so we want the rules to be simple and clear, as required by the automatic renewal and consumer protection laws of the District of Columbia, Maryland and Virginia.

1. Your consent

Before your first recurring visit we send you a quote by email showing the service, the frequency, the price per visit, and a link to these terms. Your plan begins only when you press Confirm on that quote, which is your affirmative consent to the recurring service. We then send a confirmation email that you can keep for your records.

2. Price and billing

  • The recurring price applies while you keep the same frequency, home size and task list. Adding rooms or extra tasks may change the price; we tell you before the visit.
  • Each visit is billed after it is completed. An authorization hold may be placed the day before, as explained in our Cancellation Policy.
  • Any applicable sales tax is shown on the quote and invoice.
  • If we offer a first-visit discount or promotion, the quote shows the regular price that applies afterwards.

3. Changes to price or terms

We email you at least 14 days before any price increase or material change to these terms, stating the new price or change and the date it takes effect. If you do not agree, you can cancel before that date at no cost. If you keep your next visit after the effective date, the new price applies.

4. How to cancel your plan

You can cancel at any time, using whichever method is easiest for you:

  • Reply to any of our emails, or write to bookings@luxuriapure.com with the subject "Cancel my recurring plan";
  • Call or text +1 (443) 535-7785;
  • Use the Cancel button in the email for an upcoming visit and tell us you want to end the whole plan.

We confirm the cancellation by email within one business day. Cancelling the plan has no fee. Only a visit that is already within 48 hours of its start time follows the normal fees in our Cancellation Policy.

5. Skipping, moving or pausing

You can skip or move a visit, or pause the plan (for example while travelling), with at least 48 hours' notice at no cost. If a plan is paused for more than 60 days we may treat it as cancelled; we will email you first.

6. When we may end a plan

We may end a recurring plan with at least 14 days' notice (or immediately in the cases listed in section 9 of our Terms of Service, such as unsafe conditions or harassment of our team). You pay only for visits already completed.

7. Reminders

We email a reminder a few days before each visit so that you always know the next date and can change it within the free period.

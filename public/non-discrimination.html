Non-Discrimination & Respect Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

1. Equal service for every customer

Luxuria Pure offers its services to everyone in our service area on the same terms. We do not refuse, limit or price a service differently because of a person's actual or perceived race, color, religion, national origin, sex, pregnancy, sexual orientation, gender identity or expression, age, marital or familial status, personal appearance, disability, genetic information, political affiliation, source of income, place of residence or business, military or veteran status, or any other characteristic protected by law.

This commitment follows the D.C. Human Rights Act, Maryland's anti-discrimination laws, Article 4 of the Baltimore City Code, the Virginia Human Rights Act and federal civil rights law, including the Americans with Disabilities Act. Our quotes depend only on the service requested, home size, condition, frequency and location.

2. Our team

We hire, pay, train and promote our team members based on their skills and performance, without discrimination on any protected basis. Background checks are performed only after a conditional offer, by a consumer reporting agency, in line with the federal Fair Credit Reporting Act and the fair-chance hiring laws of D.C. (Fair Criminal Record Screening Amendment Act), Maryland, Baltimore City and Virginia.

Customers may not request, and we will not honor requests for, a cleaner of a particular race, religion, national origin, sex, age or other protected characteristic.

3. A safe and respectful home visit

Our cleaners work alone or in small teams inside private homes, so their safety matters. During a visit, customers and anyone in the home must not:

  • harass, threaten, insult or make sexual comments or advances toward our team;
  • touch a team member without consent, or block their way out of a room or the home;
  • record our team in private areas, or ask them to work in unsafe conditions (see Terms of Service);
  • ask for services outside the booking, or offer cash to skip company procedures.

Team members may stop a visit and leave if they feel unsafe. In that case the visit may be charged as a lock-out, and we may end the service relationship. Serious incidents may be reported to the police.

Our team, in turn, must treat you, your family, your guests and your home with courtesy and respect. Discriminatory or disrespectful behavior by a team member is a serious breach of our rules.

4. Accommodations

If you need an accommodation to book or receive our services (for example, communication by text instead of phone, a team that is told in advance about a service animal, or extra time to give access), tell us at booking or write to bookings@luxuriapure.com. See also our Accessibility Statement.

5. How to report a concern

If you believe you were treated unfairly, or you want to report behavior by anyone involved in a visit, email info@luxuriapure.com with the subject "Respect Policy Report", or call +1 (443) 535-7785. We review every report promptly and confidentially, and we do not retaliate against anyone who reports in good faith.

You may also contact the agency for your area:

  • Washington, D.C.: D.C. Office of Human Rights — ohr.dc.gov
  • Maryland: Maryland Commission on Civil Rights — mccr.maryland.gov
  • Baltimore City: Baltimore City Office of Equity and Civil Rights (Community Relations Commission)
  • Virginia: Office of the Attorney General, Office of Civil Rights — oag.state.va.us

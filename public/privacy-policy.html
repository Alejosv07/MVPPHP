3. How we use information

  • To review your request, prepare quotes, schedule, perform and follow up on services.
  • To send transactional messages: quotes, confirmations, reminders, status updates, receipts and security PINs.
  • To process payments, prevent fraud and keep our website and accounts secure (for example, limiting repeated login or PIN attempts).
  • To handle quality issues, damage claims, and to improve our services.
  • To send marketing messages only if you have agreed; you can opt out at any time.
  • To comply with legal, tax and accounting obligations.

4. How we share information

We share personal information only as needed to run our business:

  • Our cleaning team: the staff assigned to your appointment see your name, phone number, address, home details and access instructions.
  • Service providers acting on our behalf under confidentiality obligations: website hosting, email delivery, payment processing and mapping (address lookup through OpenStreetMap's Nominatim service).
  • Website resources: our pages load fonts and scripts from Google Fonts, the Tailwind CSS CDN and unpkg; these providers receive your IP address as part of delivering the files.
  • Legal reasons: when required by law, to respond to valid legal process, or to protect the safety of our clients, staff or the public.
  • Business transfer: if our business is sold or merged, information may transfer to the new owner, who must honor this policy.

Mobile phone numbers and SMS opt-in data are never shared with third parties or affiliates for their marketing purposes.

5. Cookies and similar technologies

We do not use advertising or analytics cookies. Our staff portal stores a session token in your browser's local storage so team members stay signed in; it is removed when they sign out or the session expires. We honor Global Privacy Control (GPC) browser signals as a request to opt out of any sale or targeted advertising, although we do neither.

6. How long we keep information

  • Booking, invoice and payment records: up to 7 years, for tax and accounting purposes.
  • Access codes and key information: deleted within 30 days after our service relationship ends.
  • Security PINs and password reset codes: expire after 10–15 minutes and are single-use.
  • Security logs: up to 12 months.
  • Reviews: until you ask us to remove them.

7. Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS), access controls so staff see only their own assignments, hashed passwords, limits on repeated login and PIN attempts, and restricted access to access codes. No system is completely secure; if a breach affects your personal information, we will notify you and the relevant authorities as required by the District of Columbia (D.C. Code § 28-3851 et seq.), Maryland (Md. Code, Com. Law § 14-3501 et seq.) and Virginia (Va. Code § 18.2-186.6) data breach laws.

8. Your rights and choices

Wherever you live, you can ask us to:

  • Confirm whether we hold your personal information and give you a copy in a portable format;
  • Correct inaccurate information;
  • Delete your information (we may keep records we are legally required to keep);
  • Stop marketing emails or texts (use the unsubscribe link or reply STOP);
  • Remove a review you submitted.

How to make a request: email privacy@luxuriapure.com or call +1 (443) 535-7785. To protect you, we verify requests by sending a code to the email address in our records. You may use an authorized agent with your signed permission. We respond within 45 days (extendable once by another 45 days when reasonably necessary, with notice). We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

Appeals: if we decline your request, you can appeal by replying with the subject line "Privacy Appeal". We will answer within 60 days and explain our decision.

9. Maryland, Virginia and District of Columbia residents

Maryland

Maryland residents have rights under the Maryland Online Data Privacy Act, including the rights to confirm, access, correct, delete and obtain a copy of personal data, to obtain a list of categories of third parties to which data was disclosed, and to opt out of targeted advertising, the sale of personal data and profiling. We extend these rights to you as described above. If your appeal is denied, you may contact the Maryland Attorney General's Consumer Protection Division.

Virginia

Virginia residents have rights under the Virginia Consumer Data Protection Act to access, correct, delete and obtain a copy of personal data and to opt out of targeted advertising, sale and profiling. If your appeal is denied, you may contact the Office of the Attorney General of Virginia.

District of Columbia

D.C. residents may exercise all of the rights listed in section 8. Questions about consumer protection may also be directed to the Office of the Attorney General for the District of Columbia.

10. Children

Our website and services are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or, for any sale or targeted advertising, under 18). If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Accessibility Statement

Last updated: November 8, 2026

Our commitment

Luxuria Pure wants everyone to be able to learn about and book our services, including people with disabilities. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA, consistent with the Americans with Disabilities Act and applicable D.C., Maryland and Virginia laws.

What we do

  • Use semantic page structure, readable text sizes and sufficient color contrast.
  • Make forms usable with a keyboard and label every field.
  • Provide text alternatives for meaningful images.
  • Review the site when we add new features.

Known limitations

The interactive service-area map relies on a third-party mapping library and may be difficult to use with a screen reader. The same information is available in text on our website, and our team can confirm by phone or email whether we serve your address.

Book another way

If anything on our website is hard to use, you can book or change an appointment by phone or text at +1 (443) 535-7785 or by email at bookings@luxuriapure.com. You will receive the same prices and availability.

Feedback

Please tell us about any accessibility barrier at info@luxuriapure.com. We aim to respond within 2 business days.

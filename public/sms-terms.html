SMS Terms

Last updated: November 8, 2026

1. Program

Luxuria Pure Appointment Alerts sends text messages about your bookings: confirmations, reminders, arrival ("on the way") notices, schedule changes and replies to messages you send us.

2. Consent

  • You join by checking the SMS box on our booking form or by texting us first. By opting in, you agree to receive automated text messages from Luxuria Pure at the number you provided.
  • Consent is not a condition of purchase. You can book and receive services without texts.
  • Promotional texts are sent only if you separately give express written consent to receive them.

3. Message frequency and cost

Message frequency varies, typically 1–5 messages per booking. Message and data rates may apply according to your mobile plan.

4. How to stop or get help

  • Reply STOP (or CANCEL, END, QUIT, UNSUBSCRIBE) at any time to stop messages. You will receive one final message confirming your opt-out, and we will honor it promptly.
  • Reply HELP for help, or contact info@luxuriapure.com or +1 (443) 535-7785.
  • To join again, check the SMS box on a new booking or text START.

5. Carriers

Wireless carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Supported carriers include the major U.S. carriers; availability may vary.

6. Privacy

We do not sell, rent or share your mobile number or SMS opt-in information with third parties or affiliates for marketing purposes. See our Privacy Policy.

7. Quiet hours

Except for messages about an appointment taking place that day, we send texts only between 8:00 a.m. and 8:00 p.m. local time.

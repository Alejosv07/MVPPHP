Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about booking and working with Luxuria Pure.

Do I need to be home during the cleaning? expand_more
No, you do not need to be home. Most of our clients provide us with a spare key, door code, or lockbox access. We are fully insured and our cleaners are thoroughly background-checked. If you prefer to be home, that is perfectly fine too!
Do you bring your own cleaning supplies? expand_more
Yes, our teams arrive fully equipped with all necessary cleaning supplies and professional-grade equipment, including vacuums and mops. If you have specific products you prefer we use (e.g., specific hardwood floor cleaner), just let us know and leave them out for us.
What is your cancellation or rescheduling policy? expand_more
We require 48 hours notice for cancellations or rescheduling to avoid a fee. Cancellations made within 48 hours of the appointment will incur a 50% charge, and same-day cancellations or lock-outs will be charged the full amount.
Are your cleaners vetted and insured? expand_more
Absolutely. We only hire top-tier professionals who pass comprehensive national and local background checks. Luxuria Pure is also fully bonded and insured for up to $2 million in liability, giving you complete peace of mind.
What if I am not satisfied with the clean? expand_more
We offer a Luxuria Pure Quality Guarantee. If you are not completely satisfied with any part of your clean, contact us within 24 hours of your service, and we will return to re-clean the missed area at no additional charge.
Do you clean with eco-friendly products? expand_more
We use professional-grade, safe cleaning solutions. If you prefer a completely green/eco-friendly cleaning, please select that option during booking. We can accommodate eco-friendly requests using trusted plant-based products.
How is payment handled? expand_more
Payment is processed securely online via credit or debit card. A hold is placed on your card the day before service, but you are not actually charged until the cleaning is completed. We do not accept cash or checks.
